The game I propose to make in my custom game engine, DeO3D, will have a rather uninspired name, Dehumanizing Obelisk, exactly like the full name of the engine apart from the DeO abbreviation. At its core, it isan open world game with environments consisting of barren steppes, valleys, utterly devastated urban areas, and destroyed military outposts, bunkers, etc.

BACKGROUND:
    So, to begin, the player is a mere initiate in something called the FNCDD: FapNation Coastal Defense Division. They're holed up in a weapons depot carved into the side of a mountain after recieving some head trauma from getting in a fight with a drunkard at a bar. But why are they in some random bunker for medical treatment? Because there is an ongoing, brutal war between two of the final standing nations on Earth. These nations are of course, FapNation, and another named Agartha. There is another nation named Blackland that does not engage in the war whatsoever. Nearly all medical facilities are currently completely filled with soldiers from FapNation. But what is this war for you may ask? Well, global warming has become SO extreme that all other nations beside these three are completely uninhabitable, and sources of water, be that fresh or salt water, are drying up fast, with what remains being heavily adulterated by pollution and industrial runoff.
